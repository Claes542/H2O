Hole on a hydrogen chain
Five H kernels, but only four electrons: the leftmost proton is bare. The chain carries charge +1 and the vacancy starts on the left.
Why a hole and not an extra electron. An added electron has to be bound, and H− barely is — in electron_chain the excess was simply expelled from the chain, which may say more about hydrogen's electron affinity than about conduction. A hole needs no affinity: it is an absence, so nothing has to hold it.
The question. Do the four electron domains rearrange so that the vacancy moves along the chain — electronic charge transport by transfer, the counterpart of Grotthuss — or do they simply expand into the gap and leave the vacancy where it was?
Migrating → the framework transports electronic charge. Static → carriers must make room for themselves, which is not how a metal works. Spacing sepAU is the dial.

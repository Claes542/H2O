Excess electron on a hydrogen chain
The electron counterpart of proton_chain. Five H atoms in a line; one extra electron seeded on the leftmost as a ghost domain (Z=1, Z_nuc=0).
The question. Grotthuss conduction shows this framework carries charge by transfer between localised sites when the carrier is a proton. Does an electron do the same — sit on one site and hop — or does its domain spread along the chain?
Localised → hopping transport, semiconductor-like, dσ/dT > 0.
Spread → band-like, which is what a metal needs and what a theory of non-overlapping domains is not obviously able to give.
Chain spacing is the dial: wide should localise (Mott side), narrow should favour spreading (metallic side). Edit sepAU and rerun to look for a transition.

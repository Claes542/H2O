A straight chain, and one electron sliding along it
n hydrogen atoms in a line along x, doped by ionising every second one, whose electrons form an outer cylinder at standoff dr. The cylinder is slid parallel to the chain and the energy recorded. E(d) is the corrugation; its amplitude is the resistance. Neutral at every doping, since the carriers are the chain's own electrons.
Why doped rather than one loose electron. A single electron beside the chain has nothing to stop it smearing along the chain direction, and it duly does — which is what made the one-electron corona result unreadable, its energy flat because the solution had forgotten where the seed was rather than because sliding was cheap. With a carrier at every second site each has neighbours and cannot spread without paying.
Straight, not a ring. The earlier runs bent the chain into a loop so that sliding by one site spacing returned an identical configuration — an exact check, but at n=6 the loop radius equals the site spacing, so it is a tight hexagon in which each site's neighbours lie at 60° rather than along a line. This is the geometry as actually pictured: a wire.
What replaces the periodicity check. With an odd n the chain is symmetric about its centre atom, so E(−d) must equal E(d) exactly. That is a genuine check — the two are different configurations that symmetry forces to agree, not one configuration relabelled — and it is the test that caught the collapsed runs earlier. Run &d=0.5 and &d=-0.5 as a matched pair.
The ends, and how to keep them out of it. A straight chain has two, and sliding charge toward one measures an edge effect rather than transport. Keep d small so the carrier stays near the middle, and use enough atoms that the ends are several spacings away. At n=9 the carrier at d=1 still has four atoms on either side.
Charge. With ion=0 every atom keeps its electron and the extra one leaves the wire at net −1. A charged cluster in vacuum spreads for reasons unconnected with conduction, which is why an earlier flat result from this configuration was withdrawn. Setting &ion=1 instead ionises one atom, so its electron becomes the carrier and the wire stays neutral — the same experiment without that confound. Running both is the point.

Ortho-He spectrum — outer electron in V_eff(r) from inner 1s

For He: inner electron in 1s, outer in 2s/3s/.... Effective potential for outer electron:
  V_eff(r) = −Z_nuc/r + V_H(r)  where V_H is the Hartree from a hydrogenic 1s with Z_eff = Z1s.
For hydrogenic 1s of charge Z1s: V_H(r) = (1/r)·[1 − (1+Z1sr)·exp(−2Z1sr)]
→ V_eff(r) = (1/r)·[−1 − (1+Z1sr)·exp(−2Z1sr)]
At small r: V_eff → −2/r (full −Znuc/r). At large r: V_eff → −1/r (screened to net +1).
Z1s from Slater rule for He: 27/16 ≈ 1.6875 (or use ground-state RealQM result).

Energy around a ring: the corrugation is the resistance
A ring of H atoms with one extra electron in the corona just outside it. The excess is slid around by angle and the energy recorded. Flat means nothing opposes the flow; rippled means the ripple amplitude is what a carrier must overcome, which is resistance in its most elementary form — the periodic lattice corrugating the path.
Why a ring, and why this is answerable statically. Time evolution is needed to compute a rate, not to establish whether flow is possible. If the path is flat, nothing opposes it and the current is set by however fast charge is fed in and tapped off — the rate is bookkeeping. Whether the path is flat is a static question. A ring realises feed-and-tap exactly: no ends, no edge artefacts, and every site equivalent by construction.
Built-in check. By symmetry the energy must repeat every 360°/n. If the computed curve does not, the calculation is wrong — which is the validity test every earlier attempt in this line lacked.

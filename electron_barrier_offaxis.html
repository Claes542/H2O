Passing an ion: does the carrier go round?
One configuration per page load. The previous version drove the scan with activateAtoms and corrupted state between points — its y=0 row reported 17.19 Ha where the axial scan gives 1.16 Ha for the same configuration. A fresh load per point removes that failure mode entirely.
URL parameters. ?y=1.0&s=0.5 — perpendicular offset in a0, and position along the path from the interstitial region between sites 1–2 to the one between sites 2–3. The sideways step is largest at s=0.5, exactly where site 2 is passed.
The self-check. ?y=0&s=0.5 puts the carrier on site 2 and must return E ≈ −3.53, the axial scan's endpoint. If it does not, believe nothing else.

Hopping barrier for an electron
Conduction by transfer is governed by a barrier, and a barrier is a static energy difference — which this solver computes. No time evolution is needed.
Four H atoms in a line. The carrier is an extra electron, seeded as a ghost domain (Z=1, Z_nuc=0), placed at a succession of positions along the path from site 1 to site 2. Each position is relaxed to convergence and its energy recorded, tracing the potential-energy surface the carrier moves on.
Reading it.
barrier ≲ kBT (0.001 Ha at 300 K) → facile hopping, the framework conducts
barrier ⋙ kBT → the carrier is trapped, an insulator
energy flat along the path → the domain is indifferent to which kernel it sits near, which is delocalisation and the nearest thing to band behaviour a static solver can show

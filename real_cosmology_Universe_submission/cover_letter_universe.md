# Cover letter — submission to *Universe* (MDPI)

**Manuscript:** A Proton–Electron Cosmology: RealQM and Newtonian Gravitation from a Single Electric-Potential Seed
**Author:** Claes Johnson, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden
**Suggested article type:** Article (theoretical / exploratory)
**Section:** Cosmology

---

Dear Editors,

I am pleased to submit the manuscript *A Proton–Electron Cosmology: RealQM and Newtonian Gravitation from a
Single Electric-Potential Seed* for consideration as an Article in *Universe*.

The paper sets out an exploratory, non-standard cosmological model built from only two well-understood
ingredients — electron–proton Coulomb interaction and Newtonian gravitation — with no Big Bang singularity, no
inflaton, no dark-matter particle, and no cosmological constant. The single primitive is a small-scale
fluctuation of the electric potential; charge is its Laplacian, and the two-tier structure of the universe then
follows: the Laplacian's short-wavelength weighting produces a small-scale proton–electron world of nuclei and
atoms (the RealQM / RealNucleus program), and the ordinary energy/mass density of that Coulombic matter sources an
emergent Newtonian gravitation whose positive and negative curvature sectors separate into the cosmic web and a
repulsive (dark-energy-like) component. The model reproduces a helium mass fraction near Y ≈ 0.25 as a
consistency check.

The manuscript is deliberately framed as a proposal, not as established cosmology. It includes an explicit
Status note, a section relating the model to ΛCDM and to observations (stating plainly what it does and does not
yet address — the redshift–distance relation, the CMB, precise large-scale-structure statistics, and light
elements beyond helium), and a candid list of load-bearing open problems, chiefly what sets the nuclear energy
scale. What the model offers in exchange for its incompleteness is economy: fewer free primitives than the
standard account.

I believe the work fits the scope of *Universe*, which welcomes original and exploratory theoretical
contributions to cosmology and foundational physics, including approaches outside the mainstream that are argued
carefully and honestly. The paper is self-contained and does not presuppose familiarity with the author's earlier
RealQM and RealNucleus work, which is cited for context.

The manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal.
The author has no competing interests to declare. There are no funding sources to acknowledge and no data sets
associated with the work (it is analytical/theoretical; supporting interactive simulations are freely available
online and are cited).

Thank you for considering the manuscript. I would be happy to respond to any questions.

Yours sincerely,

## Notes for the submission portal (not part of the letter)

- **APC:** Universe is open-access; the article processing charge (~2200 CHF) applies **only on acceptance**,
  not at submission or review. Decide this is acceptable before committing to Universe as the target.
- **Format:** MDPI recommends its own LaTeX (`universe`/`mdpi` class) but accepts a well-prepared PDF for the
  initial submission; the template is required only in production after acceptance. The current
  `real_cosmology_article.pdf` is acceptable for first submission. (Ask me to convert to the MDPI template if you
  prefer to submit in it from the start.)
- **Suggested reviewers (supply 3–5).** Rather than invent contacts, propose people by expertise and fill in
  current affiliations/emails yourself:
  - a researcher in **emergent / entropic gravitation** (gravity as a large-scale consequence rather than a
    fundamental field);
  - a **foundations-of-cosmology / non-standard cosmology** specialist comfortable with paradigm-level
    proposals;
  - a **nuclear / few-body Coulomb** theorist who can assess the RealNucleus proton–electron claims;
  - optionally, an **electromagnetism / classical-field** theorist for the two-valued-Poisson machinery.
  Avoid anyone at your own institution or a recent collaborator (MDPI excludes these).
- **Opposed reviewers:** you may name any strict ΛCDM-orthodox referee you expect to reject on paradigm grounds.
- **Cover-letter tone:** kept honest and non-combative on purpose — a heterodox paper survives review far better
  when it concedes its limits up front than when it oversells.
